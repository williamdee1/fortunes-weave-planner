## Notes

- **Late arrivals in Dagsion:** Benditz (~4/11), Kiroc (~5/4), Jasmine (~5/8), Alexandra (~5/29), Nuzzuo (~7/1), Inyoni (~7/10).
- **Item locations:** Giant's Meat is always found in Sand-Shadow Fort, or in skirmishes with a Giant icon. Paradise Fish is easiest at Lake Brontes in Saveilon. Glimrosa is found at Mount Orgus Trail, Rear Mountain Path, Forest of No Return, or Giant's Mount in Pasithea.
- **Support is global across your save file.** It never decreases, even if you restart a chapter, and it carries over between paths.
- **Unverified story chapters:** the chapter numbers for story auto-recruits (e.g. Bonaventure Ch. 3, Esmeralda Ch. 4, Fabio Ch. 2, the Ch. 1 joiners) were kept from the original table but couldn't be confirmed.
- † **Contested between guides:** Raider King lists 2x Bronze Axes for Nydine on all routes, and Support 1 / Renown 9 for Yang Jie on Theodora's route. Esmeralda on Cai's route is listed as Support 2 by Raider King but Support 3 by RPG Site and GamesHedge.
